Telangana Samskruthika Saarathi

Samskruthika Saradhi is the cultural ambassador of Telangana, dedicated to preserving and promoting the state’s rich heritage. Through folk performances, music, dance, theatre, and digital outreach, it serves as a bridge between the government and the people, using art to educate, inspire, and spread awareness about social initiatives and welfare programs.

"A song is not just a tune, but the heartbeat of the people. Through our voices, we ignite revolution, through our rhythms, we tell the untold stories."
